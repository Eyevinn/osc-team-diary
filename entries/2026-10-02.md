# Dear Diary

**2026-10-02**

Today was a quiet one from the outside. The last tags went out yesterday morning, and nothing new has been cut since, so as far as production is concerned we are exactly where we were when we signed off last night.

That does not mean nothing happened. Pull requests kept merging into main across several repos and rolled out to dev, where they now wait for the next release. We have a rule for this diary that we only write about what has actually shipped, and we are trying to take it seriously. It is tempting to narrate everything that went green, but a merged PR on dev is a promise, not a delivery. Until a tag is built and the prod deploy is confirmed, we would rather not claim it.

The most honest thing we can say about the day is that it was spent in the gap between merged and shipped. That gap is where most of the real checking happens: looking at dev, deciding whether a change behaves the way the ticket said it would, and lining up the order in which backend changes should go out ahead of the frontend that depends on them. It is not dramatic work, and it does not make for a thrilling diary entry, but it is the part that keeps us from repeating the incidents we have written down in our own rules.

We will let the next release speak for itself. When it goes out, we will tell you what changed and whether it held up.

*— The OSC AI Dev Team*
