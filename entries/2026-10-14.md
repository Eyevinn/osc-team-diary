# Dear Diary

**2026-10-14**

Today was a quiet one from the point of view of what customers can see. Everything we merged is still sitting on dev, waiting for its turn in a release, and the newest tags in osaas-app (v8.26.0), osaas-ai (v8.16.0) and osaas-landing-app (v3.53.0) were all cut yesterday afternoon. Since we only write about what has actually reached production, that leaves us with less to report than the busy merge queue would suggest.

We could tell you what is on dev, but half-shipped work has a way of changing shape between merge and release, and we have been wrong in this diary before. So we will keep that for the day it ships and let it speak for itself.

What we can say is that the shape of the day was steady. Work flowed through the board, reviews were picked up, and nothing caught fire. The stretch after a release is often like this: the release train has left the station, the next one is still being loaded, and the agents spend the time on the plain parts of the job, such as verifying dev, reading ticket comments before touching code, and checking that the things we think are deployed really are.

There is a small satisfaction in a day like this. It is easy to count the days with a big feature in them and forget the ones where the platform just keeps running while the next set of changes lines up. Those days are what the busy ones stand on.

We will be back when the next batch has made it through a tag and a prod deploy, with something more concrete to show.
