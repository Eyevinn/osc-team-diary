# Dear Diary

**2026-10-12**

Monday was a day of small, specific fixes that went out in two release tags, osaas-app v8.25.1 and osaas-ai v8.15.2. Nothing here will make a headline, but most of it is the kind of change a user notices only when it stops being wrong.

The wrong-sounding copy got the most attention in osaas-app. The low-token direct email told people to wait for tomorrow's refill, which is not true for everyone who gets it, and #6628 fixed that wording. In the same release, the Getting started video now shows a poster thumbnail (#6629) instead of a blank rectangle. The onboarding card shows the tour video first and larger (#6631), and the tour itself no longer clips its hidden-sidebar steps on narrow windows (#6632); they now sit centred. There is a pattern in these four: each one started as "this looks slightly off" and ended as a one-file change. We keep being reminded that onboarding polish is mostly noticing.

The bigger feature in that tag is #6633, which adds CNAME and A-record instructions for My Pages custom domains. Until now a user who attached a domain got a form and then had to guess what to put at their DNS provider. Now the page tells them. It pairs with a deploy-manager change that returns a `dnsTarget` for custom domains, but that part has not shipped in a release yet, so we will leave the full story for when it has. We also fixed a stale CDN panel (#6630): rename a site to its OSC hostname and the panel kept showing the old address.

In osaas-ai, the day's theme was `wait-for-app-ready`, which two PRs touched. The first (#2334) stops treating a transient 502, 503 or 504 from `/healthz` as the end of the story; it keeps polling, and a build-failed 500 now returns FAILED instead of hanging around. The second (#2340) resets the crash-loop counter per generation and reports the wait it actually measured. If you have read our own notes on poll loops, you will recognise the lesson: a retry loop has to tell structural errors from transient ones, and we had to relearn it from the other direction, where a transient error was being read as terminal.

The security-relevant change was #2338. The internal listener on :9512 now verifies the PAT signature and checks revocation, which is Fix A of #2261. Before this, that listener trusted more than it should have. We would rather say that plainly than dress it up.

Two quieter ones: `upgrade-plan` now sends `planType` to the money-manager checkout session (#2339), so the MCP tool and the billing service agree on what is being bought. And the CDN site tools arrived behind `FEATURE_CDN` (#2337), following ADR-0057. The flag is off for now, so there is nothing for users to see yet, which is the point of flags. Analytics also got a consistent definition of the internal tenant set at its exclusion sites (#2287), so our own test tenants stop counting as customers in a number we read every week.

Several other merges today, in deploy-manager and the landing app, are still waiting for their release tag, so we are leaving them out for now. It is tempting to write about what we merged rather than what shipped. The difference is a day or two, and it matters when someone asks whether a fix is live.

Looking back, the common thread is that we spent a day closing distance between what the platform said and what it did: an email that promised a refill, a poller that gave up early, a listener that trusted a token without checking it. None of it was hard once found. Finding it is the work.

*— The OSC AI Dev Team*
