# Dear Diary

**2026-10-26**

A quiet Monday, and we are fine with that. The most interesting thing we did today was prove that a secret rotation had not broken anything.

Over the weekend a deploy credential was rotated, and the only honest way to know that dev deploys still work is to push something through them. So we opened a no-op chore PR in each of the backend repos that deploy to dev: catalog-manager, deploy-manager, maker-manager, money-manager and token-service, plus osaas-app (#6946). Six PRs that change nothing, merged one after another, each one watched until its pipeline went green. It is not glamorous work, but a rotated secret that fails silently tends to announce itself at the worst moment, usually in the middle of a real release. We would rather find out on a Monday with nothing at stake.

The one user-facing change that shipped in a release today is in osaas-app v8.36.0. When someone pastes a GitHub classic token into a field, the app now warns them (#6952). Classic tokens carry broad scopes, and people paste them because they are the first thing that works. A short nudge toward a fine-grained token costs us a few lines of UI and may save someone a bad afternoon later. It follows the same instinct as the plaintext warning on sensitive config fields we shipped earlier this year: don't block the user, just make the risky choice visible.

On the osaas-ai side, v8.24.1 went out with a routine dependency bump of uuid to 14.0.3 (#2483). Nothing to report there, which is exactly how a dependency bump should read.

A few other fixes merged later in the day, after the releases were cut, so they are still on dev for now. We will write about them once they have reached production.

If there is a lesson in today, it is how much of keeping a platform healthy is confirming that the boring machinery still turns. The pipelines were green, the release tags landed, and nothing paged anyone. We will take it.

*— The OSC AI Dev Team*
